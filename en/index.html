    FAQEverything developers ask us

    01.

    Is there a monthly fee or hosting charge?

    No. Hosting, maintenance and updates are covered by the one-time payment until the project is sold out. Once it sells, you’re done.

    02.

    Can we start with one apartment?

    Yes. The ₪990 pilot covers one unit type, and if you move on to a package, the full amount is credited.

    03.

    Who owns the model?

    The model is built for your project, and you can use it across every marketing channel until sales close.

    01.

    What do you need from us?

    The sales floor plans for each unit type, as PDF or DWG. If you have renderings, a logo and brand guidelines, we’ll match the model to them.

    02.

    How long does it take?

    The first unit type is usually ready within a few business days of receiving the plans. A full project depends on the number of unit types.

    03.

    What if the floor plan changes?

    We update the model. Floor plan changes and availability updates during the sales campaign are included.

    04.

    Can we choose the furniture and style?

    Yes. The furniture is tailored to your project’s audience and your brand style.

    01.

    Is it a realistic rendering?

    The base model is a clean architectural model that explains the apartment: dimensions, layout and flow. The Signature package also adds a realistic mode with materials and lighting, like in the Marc Chagall project.

    02.

    Does it work on mobile?

    Yes. It’s built mobile-first and works in any modern browser, with nothing to install.

    03.

    Do buyers need to download an app?

    No. It’s a regular link that opens in the browser, from WhatsApp, email or your website.

    04.

    Who records the narration?

    A professional English voice actor, from a script we write based on the project specs and you approve.

    01.

    Where is it shown?

    On a dedicated link, embedded on the project website, in landing pages and campaigns, and on the sales office screen.

    02.

    Can we embed it on our website?

    Yes. You get a simple embed code, and the model opens right inside your project page.

    03.

    How do inquiries reach us?

    From a form inside every apartment, to WhatsApp, email or straight into your CRM, with the name of the apartment the buyer viewed.
